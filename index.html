The roadmap

Why each step matters

Every step in the app comes with a reason, so you're never just ticking boxes.

Phase 1

Radical Acceptance

Write down the facts of what ended.

Why this step matters: Plain facts stop the mind from bargaining with "what if." You can't move away from something you're still negotiating with.

Name three feelings out loud.

Why this step matters: Putting a name on a feeling lowers its intensity and makes it something you can work with, not something that runs you.

Say "This chapter is over, and I will be okay."

Why this step matters: Hearing your own voice say it begins turning a painful idea into an accepted fact.

Phase 2

Processing Without Spiraling

Set a daily 15-minute "feelings window."

Why this step matters: Giving grief a time and place keeps it from leaking into every hour of your day.

Write an unsent letter — and keep it unsent.

Why this step matters: You get to say everything without consequences, so the words stop circling in your head.

Tell one trusted person how you really are.

Why this step matters: Carrying it alone makes it heavier. One honest conversation lightens more than a week of coping in silence.

Phase 3

Boundaries & Detox

Mute, archive, or hide their messages and updates.

Why this step matters: Every reminder restarts the craving to check. Less exposure means fewer urges to fight.

Box up reminders for 30 days.

Why this step matters: Out of sight gives your nervous system room to settle. Nothing is thrown away — you're just creating space.

Write your own "if I feel the urge" plan.

Why this step matters: Decisions made while calm beat decisions made at 11 p.m. A plan you wrote yourself is easier to follow.

Phase 4

Rebuilding Your Identity

List 5 things that are yours alone.

Why this step matters: Relationships can blur who you are. Reconnecting with your own values and interests rebuilds a steady sense of self.

Schedule one thing just for you this week.

Why this step matters: Moving on is built from actions, not intentions. A booked plan turns "someday" into "Saturday."

Write a note from you, 90 days from now.

Why this step matters: Picturing an ordinary good day ahead gives your mind something to walk toward, not just away from.

Good to know

How do I receive it?

After I verify your receipt, I send you a private link to your tracker plus your unlock code, usually by email or message. Open the link, enter the code once, and you're in.

How do I add it to my iPhone Home Screen?

Open your link in Safari (not inside another app's built-in browser). Tap the Share button, scroll down, then tap Add to Home Screen and confirm.

How do I add it to my Android Home Screen?

Open your link in Chrome. Tap the Menu ⋮, then choose Install app or Add to Home screen, and confirm.

Can I share my link with friends?

Your link and unlock code are meant for you. If a friend could use it, please send them to this page so they can get their own copy — it also helps keep this project going.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your own phone, in your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server. That also means clearing your browser data can erase it, so use the built-in Copy / Restore Backup box now and then, especially before switching phones.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After your first visit, the tracker is saved for offline use, so you can open it any time from your Home Screen.

Is this therapy?

No. It's a self-guided companion for everyday heartbreak and transitions. If you ever feel unsafe or overwhelmed, please reach out to a trusted person or a licensed mental health professional in your area.
