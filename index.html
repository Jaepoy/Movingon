The roadmap

Why each step matters

Every step comes with a reason, so you're never just ticking boxes. And you never have to write anything — you can think it or say it out loud.

Phase 1

Radical Acceptance

Say out loud, just to yourself: "It's over, and I'm learning to accept that."

Why this step matters: Hearing your own voice say it helps your mind start treating it as real. Acceptance begins when you stop arguing with what already happened.

Think back and tell yourself what really happened between you two. Just the facts, no "what ifs."

Why this step matters: When you stay with the plain facts, your mind has less room to replay a happier version that never fully existed.

Name how you feel right now, out loud. Sad? Angry? Lonely? Relieved?

Why this step matters: Naming a feeling makes it smaller and easier to handle. Feelings with no name tend to run the show.

Phase 2

Processing Without Spiraling

Pick one time each day to let yourself think and feel about them. Outside that time, tell yourself: "Not now, later."

Why this step matters: Giving sadness its own time stops it from taking over your whole day. You are not ignoring it — you are scheduling it.

Say what you wish you could tell them, out loud or as a voice note to yourself. Do not send it.

Why this step matters: You get to say everything with no consequences. Once it is out of your head, it stops going in circles.

Talk to one person you trust about how you're really doing.

Why this step matters: Carrying it alone makes it heavier. One honest talk can lift more than a week of keeping it inside.

Phase 3

Boundaries & Detox

Mute, hide, or unfollow them so you don't see their posts or messages.

Why this step matters: Every reminder sets off the urge to check. Less to see means less to fight each day.

Put things that remind you of them out of sight for 30 days.

Why this step matters: Out of sight gives your mind room to calm down. Nothing is thrown away — you're just making space.

Make your own rule and say it out loud: "When I feel like reaching out, I will ___ instead."

Why this step matters: A decision made when you're calm is easier to keep than one made late at night.

Phase 4

Rebuilding Your Identity

Think of 3 things you like about yourself that have nothing to do with them. Say them out loud.

Why this step matters: Relationships can blur who you are. Remembering your own strengths rebuilds a steady sense of self.

Pick one thing just for you this week and decide when you'll do it.

Why this step matters: Moving on is built from actions, not just intentions. A plan with a day and time turns "someday" into "Saturday."

Picture yourself 3 months from now, doing well. Describe that day out loud to yourself.

Why this step matters: Picturing a good future gives your mind something to walk toward, not just something to run from.
